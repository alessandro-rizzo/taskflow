## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Roadmap experiment: E07; risk R7; requirements NS-1 through NS-5.
Outcome: run two simultaneous W3 service subsets to determine whether typed, authorization-aware endpoints hide port/provider networking and mutable service state from project code.
Stable assumptions: a fake macOS consumer is allowed; E07 does not create production Service, Endpoint, or Stack packages.
Parallelism: allowed beside later core Risk Lab work once W3/lease fixtures are hardened. Own experiments/e07-namespace-services only.
Test or fault scenario: concurrent namespaces, port/data/name isolation, readiness without sleeps, unauthorized routing, caller loss, TTL cleanup, artifact reuse without mutable-data reuse, and provider-specific routing.
Observability: namespace/resource/lease IDs, routing decision and capability, health transitions, cleanup latency, collisions, and diagnostics.
Rollback or removal: all service resources are bounded to experiment namespaces with TTL and explicit teardown.
Versioned formats: endpoint/service evidence schemas are experimental.
