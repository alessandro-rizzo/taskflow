## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Roadmap experiment: E05; risks R6 and R7; requirements CONC-1 through CONC-4 and DUR-1 through DUR-4.
Outcome: simulate whether a shared daemon improves multi-agent utilization without oversubscription, starvation, lost state, or unacceptable operational complexity.
Stable assumptions: fake local, Linux, macOS, simulator, and device providers only; no real builds or production daemon.
Parallelism: first Risk Lab wave beside authoring, sandbox, and macOS feasibility. Own experiments/e05-daemon-simulation only.
Test or fault scenario: 20 mixed agents, competing priorities, saturated finite resources, restarts during admission/execution/cleanup, disconnected clients, authorized attachment, and concurrency-group queue/supersede/cancel.
Observability: resource usage, queue-time distribution, starvation, committed event continuity, orphan/cleanup latency, throughput, install/upgrade complexity, and idle overhead.
Rollback or removal: simulator is disposable and cannot become the T4 daemon implementation.
